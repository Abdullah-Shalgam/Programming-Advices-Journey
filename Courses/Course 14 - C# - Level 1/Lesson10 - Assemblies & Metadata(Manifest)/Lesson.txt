🎯 يعني إيه Assembly؟

 🔥Assemblies: Single unit ex: (.exe file).

(code + Config + Assembly)  ➡️ Compile  ➡️ Assembly (single unit of Deployment). exe or .dll

📌without Assembly you can't make deployment

💡dll = dynamic link library. (Library Assembly)
💡exe = entry-point assembly (executable process)


💥 MetaData (Mainfest) 💥
contains information about the assembly such as version, dependencies, and modules

and it is important because CLR uses metadata to understand MSIL and execute it correctly
