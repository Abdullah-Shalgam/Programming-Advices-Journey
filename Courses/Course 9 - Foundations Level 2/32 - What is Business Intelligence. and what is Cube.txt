Simple Summary

Business Intelligence (BI)
BI transforms raw data into valuable information for decision making.

Common Company Problems:
Data is distributed across the enterprise
Data everywhere, information nowhere
Reports take too long
Different users need different formats

Data Cube?
A data structure for analysis

Contains:
Dimensions: Customer, Product, Time
Measures: Sales, Quantity, Profit

Dashboards?
Visual representation of data
Helps management see performance quickly

Why BI?

Answer key questions:
What happened?
What is happening?
Why did it happen?
What will happen?
What do I want to happen?

BI Benefits:
Fact-based decision making
Single version of the truth
Reduced cost and wasted effort
Better operational efficiency
