Simple Summary

MAC (Media Access Control) Address:
A unique physical identifier assigned by the manufacturer to every network device (like a phone, laptop, or router).
It’s like a car’s chassis number — no two devices have the same MAC.

Purpose & Functions:

Improves network security.

Identifies each device’s connection history — when it joined or left the network.

Acts as the physical address in the network layer.

Can be used to:

Allow or block specific devices from network access.

Restrict internet usage for certain devices.

Track a device’s location or activity through the ISP.
