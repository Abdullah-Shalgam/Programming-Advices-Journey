Simple Summary

IP Address = The unique address of your device — either on a local network (LAN) or the internet (global).

Types of IP Addresses:

Public IP

Accessible by anyone on the internet.

Assigned by your ISP (Internet Service Provider) — e.g., Zain.

Used when your device connects directly to the internet.

Private IP

Used inside local networks (LANs) only.

Not reachable from outside the network.

Defined under both IPv4 and IPv6 standards.

You can use one Public IP and share it across many devices in a local network.

Static IP

Fixed — doesn’t change over time.

Commonly used for servers.

Limited in number and more expensive.

Provided permanently by your ISP.

Dynamic IP

Changes automatically from time to time.

Cheaper and easier for ISPs to manage.

Note:
An IP address can be Public or Private, and each can also be Static or Dynamic depending on how it’s assigned.
