Simple Network Summary

Network: A group of devices connected together to share data and resources.

Local Area Network (LAN):
A small network in a limited area like a home, school, or office.

Wide Area Network (WAN):
A very large network that connects many LANs together, like the Internet.

Wired Communication:
Connecting devices using cables.

Wireless Communication:
Connecting devices without cables using radio signals.

Ethernet:
A wired network technology used in LANs.
It uses Ethernet cables.

Switch:
A device that connects multiple devices together inside a LAN.

Router:
A device that connects a LAN to a WAN (the Internet).

Wireless Router:
A router that provides both wired and wireless (Wi-Fi) connections.

Wi-Fi (Wireless Fidelity):
A wireless technology that allows devices to connect to the network without cables.

Radio Signals:
Invisible waves used to send data wirelessly.

Protocol:
A set of rules that defines how devices communicate and exchange data.
