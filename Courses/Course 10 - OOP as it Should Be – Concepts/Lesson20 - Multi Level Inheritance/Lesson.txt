Simple Summary

Multi-Level Inheritance?
A derived class is inherited from another derived class
Forms a chain of inheritance: Base → Derived → Sub-Derived

Properties?
Public/protected members are inherited
Private members are not directly accessible
Constructor call order: Base → Derived → Sub-Derived
Destructor call order: Sub-Derived → Derived → Base

Example?
Person → Student → EngineeringStudent
EngineeringStudent can access members of both Student and Person (except private)
